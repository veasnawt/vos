# VOS Community Code of Conduct

VOS welcomes people who want to learn, review, test, and build operating-system software together. Our community should be technically rigorous and personally respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and considerate in issues, discussions, and code reviews.
- Critique technical ideas and evidence, not someone's identity or ability.
- Make room for newcomers and different levels of experience.
- Respect contributors' time, privacy, and attribution.
- Follow responsible-disclosure practices for security issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, disclosure of private information without consent, and sustained disruptive or abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in VOS project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other officially maintained community channels. It also applies when representing the project in public.

## Reporting and moderation

If you encounter problematic behavior, contact the maintainer privately through a contact method available on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/veasnawt). Do not post personal details or sensitive evidence publicly. For exploitable vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will review reports in context, try to protect privacy where practical, and may moderate comments, close threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation. There is no guaranteed response time. Retaliation for good-faith reports is not acceptable.

This document is a project-specific community policy; it does not imply an external organization or formal enforcement staff.
