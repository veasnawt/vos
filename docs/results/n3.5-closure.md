## Limitations explicitly accepted at closure

- **QEMU only. Physical hardware remains untested**, including physical USB,
  hot-unplug/reset-refusal behavior and other firmware. No SMP/IOMMU/Secure Boot
  verification or broader hardware certification is implied.
- **Full dynamic kernel-stack high-water proof is incomplete.** Compiler reports,
  the 7776-byte storage/bridge subset tally and runtime guards/canaries are partial
  evidence; they do not bound every nested scheduler/assembly/exception path.
- **The filesystem interface is read-only.** Trusted immutable provisioned directory
  graphs remain required; parsing and capability confinement do not authenticate
  malicious media or inode/block aliases.
- **UEFI interrupt limitations remain.** OVMF uses bounded polling; no IOAPIC,
  MSI or MSI-X was added. BIOS IRQ coverage does not imply UEFI IRQ coverage.
- Finite work and independent poll caps remain necessary with IF=0; no hard
  wall-clock/fairness guarantee is made. One CPU/one thread per process remains
  assumed. Unconfirmed reset can retain bounded driver-owned DMA quarantine.
- The original combined boot payload can still exhaust fragmented GRUB workspace.
  Future coverage growth must preserve both mandatory 128 MiB split-image gates.
